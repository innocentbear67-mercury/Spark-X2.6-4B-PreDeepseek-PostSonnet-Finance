# personal_finance_calc (profile A)

120 fictional household fixtures: income, spending, portfolio, target, mortgage, withdrawal
plan, goals, four accounts and a two-month cash-flow statement.

Families (104 masked): compounding and doubling time, real return after inflation and fees, fee
drag over 10, 20 and 30 years, mortgage payment/interest/extra-payment effect, savings rate
required, years to target, emergency cover, withdrawal balance and sequencing, required corpus,
goal gap, card payoff, statement extraction with blank cells and footnotes, definition qa,
risk/savings/fee/mortgage/withdrawal classification, five memo summaries, six tool flows with
argument repair and abstention.

Weaknesses: markdown cannot render merged cells (empty August cell plus two-month total row as
stand-in); no tax rules beyond stated assumptions; synthetic classification thresholds. Gap: no
tax-lot or multi-account fee-tier interaction.
